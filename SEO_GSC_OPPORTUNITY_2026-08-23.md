- Google recommends creating helpful, reliable, people-first content rather than content created primarily to manipulate rankings.
- Google recommends descriptive page titles and useful page content.
- Canonical URLs remain stable unless a concrete canonicalization problem is identified.
- Internal links should point users and crawlers to relevant canonical pages.
